Help us test the alpha - on your own hardware.

Alpha testing means trying an early build before everyone gets it, and telling us what works and what does not. Anyone with one of the supported devices can join as a volunteer - no experience needed, no login. You do not test everything: pick your device and we hand you one small task of about ten minutes, the part that needs testing most. Take another one when you like.

Something went wrong?

Tell us even if you are not following a plan. It takes about five minutes, and a log file from the console helps a lot.

Report a problem

What happens to your report

Each report gets an id. We read it, try to repeat it on the newest build, and only then call it a bug and fix it.

A bug is something that should work and does not. An idea is something you would like it to do - ideas are welcome, they are kept on a "User ideas" list and read when we plan the next release.

You can look up your report by its id on the status page.

Your data

What you send is stored on the project's server, read only by the project's maintainers, and used to fix problems and plan the next release. Contact details are optional and used only to ask about your report. Logs are kept only to investigate the report.
